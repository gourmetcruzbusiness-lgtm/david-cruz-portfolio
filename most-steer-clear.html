Most Steer Clear.

MOST STEER CLEAR More life. More choices.

ABOUT THE CAMPAIGN

Most people are already making positive choices.

Most Steer Clear is a youth-focused prevention campaign centered on alcohol and marijuana awareness. Its message highlights that most young people choose not to use drugs or alcohol.

Rather than relying on lectures or scare tactics, the campaign uses relatable, engaging, and creative messaging to connect with younger audiences.

CREATIVE APPROACH

Making prevention feel relevant.

My work on the campaign includes developing creative concepts, social media content, and messaging designed to make prevention approachable and engaging for young people.

CAMPAIGN FOCUS

  • Alcohol and marijuana prevention awareness
  • Youth-centered messaging and creative concepts
  • Social media content and audience engagement
  • Positive choices and future opportunities
